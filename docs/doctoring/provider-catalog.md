# Provider catalog citations (APA 7)

Status: citation record for the in-flight org-catalog / cost-performance
work. This note corrects author and DOI attributions. It does not treat that
implementation as landed on `main`.

In-text citations used by that work:

| Claim | Citation |
| --- | --- |
| Multi-upstream catalog so a router can pick a cheap/fast or deeper path | Chen et al. (2023); Ong et al. (2024); Ding et al. (2024) |
| Fugu-style `route` versus `conduct` | Sakana AI (2026) |
| TRINITY thinker / worker / verifier roles | Xu et al. (2025) |
| Conductor access lists | Nielsen et al. (2025) |

Trinity is Xu, Sun, Schwendeman, Nielsen, Cetin, and Tang (2025), not
Zhang et al. Conductor is Nielsen, Cetin, Schwendeman, Sun, Xu, and Tang
(2025), not Li, Y. et al.
