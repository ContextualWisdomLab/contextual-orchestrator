# Authoritative references

**Document state:** `research_only`

Citations use APA 7th edition and include a DOI or canonical URL. Research
motivates architecture hypotheses and evaluation contracts. It does not make
this repository an implementation of a trained coordinator, a proceedings
reprint, or a claim of product superiority.

arXiv comments that a paper is “to appear” at a conference are **not** treated
as a final published record. Until a version of record is independently
verified, those works are cited as versioned preprints.

Do not invent authors. TRINITY is Xu et al.; Conductor is Nielsen et al. Do
not attribute either work to Zhang or Li.
