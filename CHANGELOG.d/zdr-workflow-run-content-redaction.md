Fixed a `zdr_only` gap where workflow-run traces (in-memory and, when `--state-db` is configured, sqlite) and `evaluation_run` records persisted raw prompt/answer/output/judge-rationale content regardless of the requester's ZDR policy. `_replace_workflow_run` and `run_evaluation` now store a content-free copy — only content hashes, byte sizes, ids, timings, usage counts, and outcome codes — whenever `zdr_only` is active, across success, pending-verification, and failure records alike, with no change to the live response returned to the requesting caller. Added `scripts/scrub_zdr_workflow_traces.py` (dry-run by default) to scrub pre-fix rows from an existing `--state-db`.
