Fail closed on an ambiguous passthrough transport failure (read or connect timeout, reset or truncated connection, non-HTTP URL error) as a recorded, classified failure: the single-attempt tool-loop walk still never replays the request on another candidate, but it now records the candidate in the circuit breaker and raises the classified `502 provider_connection_error` instead of a bare `TimeoutError` that the HTTP handler answered with `500 internal_error` and that never reached the breaker (#1045; ContextualWisdomLab/.github#1812, 83 such responses in one Strix run, the same never-recorded route every time). HTTP statuses and DNS failures keep their existing handling. `classify_provider_failure` now also maps `http.client.HTTPException` (`IncompleteRead`, `BadStatusLine` — a connection dropped mid-read) to the retryable `provider_connection_error` instead of the opaque `api_error` default.
