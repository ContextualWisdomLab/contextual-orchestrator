Added opt-in verbose/DEBUG-level logging (`--verbose`/`-v`, or `CONTEXTUAL_ORCHESTRATOR_LOG_LEVEL`) that surfaces routing, failover, discovery, and circuit-breaker decisions already computed internally but never visible in process output before now. The `TaskOrchestrator` audit-event stream (`_append_audit_event`, already secret-free/PII-encrypted) now also emits through the standard `logging` module, gated by a second `redact_value` pass; `_failover_candidates` records a stage-by-stage funnel of why a candidate pool is the size it is (ZDR exclusions, capability/tag mismatches, an open circuit breaker); `_invoke` records a `candidate_pool_exhausted` event with the tried agent ids and final classified error kind on full pool exhaustion; circuit-breaker open/close transitions are logged; and `model_discovery.py` logs per-provider credential-skip/success/failure and the shared Models.dev fetch's retry/recovery/exhaustion. This is the evidence an `orchestrator/free` pool-exhaustion incident (see `ContextualWisdomLab/.github#1437`) needs to root-cause, instead of only an opaque final error.
