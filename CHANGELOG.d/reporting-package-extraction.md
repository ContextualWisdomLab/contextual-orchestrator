Move the commercial, sales, and buyer readiness report generators out of `TaskOrchestrator` into the new `contextual_orchestrator.reporting.commercial_reports` module. This is a mechanical move with no behaviour change: `TaskOrchestrator` keeps delegating methods with identical signatures (still wrapped by the per-request report cache), and `DEFAULT_COMMERCIAL_TARGET_VALUE_KRW` is still importable from `contextual_orchestrator.orchestrator`. `TaskOrchestrator` shrinks from 13,842 to 8,204 lines. `docs/adr/0124-incremental-domain-extraction.md` replaces the "domain code stays in `orchestrator.py`" rule with a staged extraction plan: reporting, then ports/adapters, then the workflow aggregate, then the combination domain, then budget and evaluation.
