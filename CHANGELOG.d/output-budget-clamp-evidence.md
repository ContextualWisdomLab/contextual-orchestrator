Fixed `ModelClient._clamp_agent_token_budget` silently rewriting a caller's explicit `max_tokens`/`max_completion_tokens`/`max_output_tokens` down to the served agent's published `max_output_tokens` ceiling with no way for the caller to tell (#1169). A request whose explicit budget exceeds that ceiling now carries `requested_output_tokens`, `effective_output_tokens`, and `output_budget_clamped: true` on both the orchestration trace step for that provider call and the `orchestration` object on the OpenAI-shaped chat completion response and its final SSE chunk; a request within the ceiling reports `output_budget_clamped: false` in the same places. No new numeric cap was introduced — the agent's own catalog ceiling remains the only limit enforced; see ADR 0130 for why a header-only signal or a hard 400 rejection were rejected in favor of this in-band evidence.
