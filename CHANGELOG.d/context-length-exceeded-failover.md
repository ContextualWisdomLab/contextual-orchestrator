Classify a provider's HTTP 400 context-window overflow as a request-size rejection instead of a generic hard failure: `_is_context_length_exceeded_error` recognizes OpenAI's structured `context_length_exceeded` code, the "maximum context length" / "context length ... tokens" message-text signal observed on OpenAI, vLLM, OpenRouter, and NVIDIA NIM passthrough responses, and the "prompt is too long" `invalid_request_error` signal observed on Anthropic-compatible proxies, and is OR'd into `_is_request_too_large_error` alongside the existing 413 and oversized-tool-description checks. A prompt that overflows one model's context window now fails over to the next capability-matched agent (which may have a larger window) instead of surfacing as a hard 400, and is not counted against the rejecting agent's provider or member health.
