Replaced the static Experiential Labs exclusion in `general_free_serving_candidates` and `TaskOrchestrator._is_free_agent` with one shared per-call cost signal (`contextual_orchestrator.free_serving_evidence`). `ModelClient` classifies every completed chat, passthrough, and stream response from its provider-reported `usage.cost` (documented by Experiential Labs and OpenRouter): a zero cost with an explicit `is_byok: false` is free evidence, a positive cost demotes the route out of every free selector immediately, and a missing, malformed, or BYOK cost is unknown. Experiential Labs (whose promotional free tier can overflow into billed credits) is free only with a recorded zero-cost verdict; other providers keep catalog-price admission and are demoted only by an explicit positive cost. No per-call cost response header is documented, so `REPORTED_COST_HEADER` stays unset (header parsing is fail-closed if one is configured).

Experiential Labs free candidates are nominated from the public keyless `GET https://api.experientiallabs.ai/api/models` `promotions[]` catalog (only an explicit `free: true`, never a `display_only` promotion or a `badge_style` label; `DiscoveredModel.free_promotion`), and a failed or timed-out read nominates nothing (fail-closed). A nominated route still needs a recorded zero-cost verdict to serve free; `free_serving_evidence.probe_free_candidates` gathers it with a bounded number of real probes. A 429 `free_limit_reached` / `insufficient_quota` on any call through `ModelClient` records `EXHAUSTED` and demotes the route; `PAID` and `EXHAUSTED` demotions hold until the next 00:00 UTC allowance reset (09:00 KST), after which the route is probe-due again and is re-admitted only by fresh zero-cost evidence. Requests sent with an `Idempotency-Key` (whose replies carry no `usage.cost`) neither promote nor demote. That a promotional free-tier call reports exactly `cost: 0` with `is_byok: false` is inferred from the provider docs and not yet observed on a live response.
