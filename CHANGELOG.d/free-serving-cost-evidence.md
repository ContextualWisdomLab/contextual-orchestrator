Replaced the static Experiential Labs exclusion in `general_free_serving_candidates` and `TaskOrchestrator._is_free_agent` with one shared per-call cost signal (`contextual_orchestrator.free_serving_evidence`). `ModelClient` classifies every completed chat, passthrough, and stream response from its provider-reported `usage.cost` (documented by Experiential Labs and OpenRouter): a JSON-number zero cost with an explicit `is_byok: false` is free evidence, a positive cost demotes the route out of every free selector immediately, and a missing, malformed (including numeric strings such as `"0"`), or BYOK cost is unknown. A call that does not complete with a parsed cost (transport error, non-2xx, truncated or non-JSON body, a stream that fails or is abandoned before its final usage frame) records `UNKNOWN` for Experiential Labs, and an HTTP 402 demotes it like an exhausted allowance. Experiential Labs (whose promotional free tier can overflow into billed credits) is free only with a recorded zero-cost verdict; other providers keep catalog-price admission and are demoted only by an explicit positive cost. No per-call cost response header is documented, so `REPORTED_COST_HEADER` stays unset (header parsing is fail-closed if one is configured).

Experiential Labs free candidates are nominated from the public keyless `GET https://api.experientiallabs.ai/api/models` `promotions[]` catalog (only an explicit `free: true`, never a `display_only` promotion or a `badge_style` label; `DiscoveredModel.free_promotion`), and a failed or timed-out read nominates nothing (fail-closed). A nominated route still needs a recorded zero-cost verdict to serve free; `free_serving_evidence.probe_free_candidates` gathers it with a bounded number of real probes. A 429 `free_limit_reached` / `insufficient_quota` on any call through `ModelClient` records `EXHAUSTED` and demotes the route; `PAID` and `EXHAUSTED` demotions are stored separately from the last verdict and hold until the next allowance reset after them, even if later calls record `UNKNOWN`; a zero-cost verdict also expires at the reset. The ledger places the reset at 00:05 UTC (00:00 UTC, 09:00 KST, plus a 5-minute clock-skew margin), after which the route is probe-due again and is re-admitted only by fresh zero-cost evidence. When the free allowance is already used up and the organization has credits overflow on, that probe can be billed once; the single billed call demotes the route until the next reset and is an accepted trade-off. Requests sent with an `Idempotency-Key` (whose replies carry no `usage.cost`) neither promote nor demote. That a promotional free-tier call reports exactly `cost: 0` with `is_byok: false` is inferred from the provider docs and not yet observed on a live response.
